# Bifrons's pact (garden cohort, GC-08)

Concordance garden cohort, GC-08 ([contract](concordance-garden-cohort.md#gc-08-bifrons-unit-5); Dossier 5). Bifrons, the
forty-sixth spirit, is a low mound of roots studded with stones that rises into a lean man of roots while he is spoken
to. By night he runs one root at a time from a full Verdant Bed of his holder's to a hungry one: two nutrients leave the
full bed, one reaches the hungry bed, and the other is lost along the root. When only beds with crops growing on them
could give, he asks which beds are reserves. He is the cohort's fifth garden spirit, on the same pact record, perch,
screen, roster, journal and Jade lines as Stolas, Buer, Bathin and Marax, and he stands on the ground beside the perch
as Marax does.

## Player experience

As built; none of it has yet been seen in a game.

- Understand **The Garden's Regard** (the codex's Binding category). Its entry has four pages of his, "Bifrons", "His
  Service", "Which Beds Are Reserves" and "His Price", and, after them, its last page, "Mastered".
- Keep **two awake Verdant Beds** within 8 blocks of a **Pact Perch** you placed, and use the perch. Its chooser shows a
  card for each spirit you may invite: his "Ready", or "Not yet" with "Needs two beds near". With fewer than two he does
  not come: "A root runs between two places. Show me two." Nothing is spent.
- Chosen, **roots push up beside the perch** into a low mound of roots studded with stones, two blocks out on the first
  clear side a quarter turn clockwise from you (never between you and the perch; with no side clear, on the perch). As
  the screen opens he **rises into a lean man of roots** and turns to you; when it closes he sinks back into the mound. A
  stage direction and three short beats (a Terms skip on the first two), then his terms as chips: "Runs roots from full
  to hungry beds", 19:00 to 05:00, 8 blocks, 24 a day, 2 logs every 7 days with 4 ahead, a day's grace, you and your
  party, and the standing terms; the price as stacks, **4 oak logs and 1 emerald**, each you lack marked before Accept.
  Accepting spends them once (no astrolabe, no resonance).
- **His runs.** By night, while you are within 64 blocks of the perch, he takes the hungriest awake bed of yours or your
  party's in his area that has room, and the fullest bed he may draw from that holds 24 nutrients or more and at least 3
  more than it. He measures first: he turns to the full bed with his inspect gesture and the shared sign of preparation
  rises there; at his next think the sign rises at the hungry bed and the root runs: **2 nutrients leave the full bed
  and 1 reaches the hungry one**, the other lost along the root. Nothing is made, carried or held between the beds, no
  strain is eased and no item moves. One run every two thinks, at most 24 a game day. Never a bed of a stranger's, or
  one you could not change by hand.
- **Which beds are reserves.** A full bed with an organism crop growing on it is a reserve: until you answer he draws
  only from fallow beds. When no fallow bed can give (none rich enough, or each that is refused him) and a growing bed
  could, he turns to it and weighs it, once while he stays; his kept page asks "Bed x, y, z is growing: draw from growing
  beds?", **Fallow beds only** (the safe course, first) or **Any full bed**, and the roster says he awaits your answer.
  "Fallow beds only" keeps them (his page shows "Reserves kept: n"); "Any full bed" lets him draw from them. You may
  change the answer whenever you like; once answered and with no reserve standing, his page asks "Which beds are
  reserves?" in general. He never draws from the bed under a Verdant Heart, though he may feed it.
- His kept page: today's runs as a gauge ("3/24 today"), by day "Asleep until 19:00", his last run ("Last: x, y, z →
  x, y, z", both beds; the amounts taken, given and lost in its hover). Jade gives his short note: "Nothing to run",
  "Nowhere to run it", "Asleep until night" or "Growing full beds: n", and his matter, "Full beds all growing".
- His first task: feed a Verdant Bed in his area, or the organism crop growing on one, with bone meal by hand while the
  pact is active. With two other garden spirits' first tasks it masters The Garden's Regard ("Kept Three Ways").
- 2 logs of any wood every 7 game days (up to 4 weeks paid ahead); a missed week is warned, a game day's grace follows,
  then the pact is suspended with nothing lost; an offering resumes it. Ending at the perch (asked first) or from the
  roster returns what was paid ahead once; he sinks into the ground.

## Bifrons's character

The forty-sixth spirit (1904 heading "Bifrons"). Source: the 1904 text, *The Book of the Goetia of Solomon the King*
(Mathers, translator, credited as "a Dead Hand"; Crowley, editor), entry 46, with the contract's labels: [S] wording
matched by a search over transcriptions of the 1904 text, [s] the sense confirmed, the wording not. No scan was opened,
and Gutenberg's record #72679 (de Laurence's 1916 reprint of the Mathers text) is still unread (GB18). Source phrases:
he "appeareth in the Form of a Monster" [S], and at the Exorcist's command a man's shape [s]; he makes one knowing in
"Astrology, Geometry" [S]; "He teacheth the Virtues of Precious Stones and Woods." [S]; an Earl [s]. **Jugcraft
adaptation: a root's measure ("Geometry") carries matter between beds, and "Woods" gives him roots.** The entry's line
about the dead and their graves, the nearest source for moving things to another place, is left out entirely (GC-S14;
it is quoted only in the contract's Dossier 5). The survey grounded root-running on a softened reading of that line,
"if the owner accepts" it, and offered a wood or orchard service built on "Woods" as the alternative; the cohort's
checkpoint (question 1) asks which the owner prefers. The two-stage appearance gives his shape. The Latin meaning
"two-faced" and any Janus link are not used. Not imported: the Megami Tensei Bifrons; the *Dictionnaire Infernal* plate;
any seal, conjuration, title or legion count. No source quotation is put in game: his beats are Jugcraft's words.

- **Silhouette:** a low mass of roots studded with stones, about 0.7 block high and 1.2 long, crawling on four short
  roots, two small eyes looking out of its front; spoken to, it draws in to a pedestal of roots and a lean man of roots
  stands up out of it, about 1.8 blocks tall, with a crown of root hair, a band of root at his waist and a simple face
  (two eyes and a mouth, no nose holes). Built geometry. His box is his low shape, 1.2 wide and 0.7 high, eyes at 0.5
  (GC-P107): the risen man is drawn above it, so the box differs by more than a fifth on one axis from every other
  spirit's and the workshop hand's. The contract's line-up says "0.6 low"; the mound as specified is 0.69.
- **Personality:** shy in his first shape, exact once risen; measures before he acts; dry about his own looks.
- **Beats (GC-P100, as the contract gives them):** greeting "Pardon the shape. I am Bifrons; I take a better one when
  spoken to."; service "Where one bed is full and another hungry, I run a root between them. Half is lost along it:
  that is a root's measure."; price "Two logs a week. A root must remember wood." Unit 5's specification proposed a
  stage direction before the greeting: "Roots push up out of the ground beside the perch and gather into a low mound
  studded with stones; two small eyes open in it, and as you speak it rises into a lean man of roots."
- **Beats as the magic interfaces draw them** (GC-S26: at most 18 words a line and a stage direction of at most 8, its
  own key drawn dim; decided by the session, amending GC-P100, as Marax's were): stage direction "A mound of roots stirs
  beside the perch." (8 words); greeting unchanged (14); service "From full beds to hungry ones I run roots. Half is
  lost: a root's measure." (15; the contract's is 24); price unchanged (9). The rest of the specification's direction is
  shown, not told: the roots push up and gather in his arrive gesture, his eyes open in the mound, and he rises as the
  screen opens.
- **Refusal:** "A root runs between two places. Show me two." (the opening of his blocker's whole sentence, its tip and
  narration). **Departure:** he sinks back into roots and burrows away.
- **Sign** (GC-P107): hanging roots (`minecraft:hanging_roots`), shown where he is not standing at his perch; no other
  spirit's sign (the spirits' signs stay unique).
- **Station** (GC-P105, in the base): unit 3's standing station, `Station.BESIDE`, with no station code of his own. He
  keeps the ground two blocks out from the perch on the first clear side, trying first the side a quarter turn clockwise
  from his inviter's (never between them and the perch), facing whoever speaks to him; on the perch's top when no side
  is clear (as in the server tests' structures, which have no sturdy floor two out).
- **Details.** (a) He rises into the man's shape only while someone speaks to him and sinks when the screen closes: his
  listening loop plays the rise once, then the risen loop, and he sinks on the main controller's blend back (GC-P109);
  by day, outside his hours, he waits low and still, unless his matter waits on an answer: then he keeps his low idle
  loop and the roster says he awaits it (AWAITING_ANSWER, GC-P47). (b) Before each run he measures, in two beats a
  think or more apart: he attends the source, and the shared preparation sign (`Sign.GATHER`, glyphs drawn in to the
  place being readied) rises there; at the next beat it rises at the destination and the root runs (GC-P102). No
  travelling sign and no bone pointing, so no client hook.
- **First task** (GC-P7, `PactObligations.Act.FEED`): a Verdant Bed, or the organism crop growing on one, fed with bone
  meal by hand (one hook line after each bone-meal success; a dispenser has no hand). With two other spirits' first
  tasks it masters The Garden's Regard.

## Service: root-running (`root_running`)

`spirits/RootRunning`; any beneficiary's beds (`party`); his own step each think by night (no on-request action).

- **Hours and quota.** The world clock's 13,000 to 23,000 (19:00 to 05:00); 24 runs a game day (`Pacts.done` counts game
  days, his hours the world clock: GC-P101). Out of his hours, or with the quota spent, a measure under way is dropped.
- **A run is two beats** (GC-P102), a think or more apart: the first chooses the pair, attends the source, plays his
  inspect gesture and shows the shared preparation sign there, and moves nothing; the second, at least `MEASURE_TICKS`
  (20) and at most `MEASURE_LIMIT` (60) ticks later, reads and asks both beds again, shows the sign at the destination,
  checks the destination has room for what reaches it (defensive: the gap already leaves room), then takes exactly
  `TAKE` (2) from the source and gives `GIVE` (1) to the destination in that one server step, counts the run once and
  plays his work gesture toward the destination. A think between them changes nothing; a measure past its limit or whose
  beds changed is dropped at no cost and made afresh.
- **The pair** (GC-P103, GC-P104): the destination is the poorest awake bed of his index with room (`Garden.poorest`),
  each asked in turn; the source the fullest bed of a beneficiary's now holding at least `SOURCE_MIN` (24) and `GAP` (3)
  more, fallow (no organism crop and no Verdant Heart on it) unless the holder answered "any full bed", and never the
  bed under a Heart. Among equals, nearest the perch first.

## Known limits

- **He may forgive Marax's proud beds,** by design: a run changes a bed's nutrients, and Marax's familiar reads a bed
  again once something there has changed; Marax never reads a fallow bed.

  again, so a reserve that filled, emptied or lost its crop since shows in Jade until his next think.
- **A slept night does not reset his quota:** his day's quota counts game days and his hours the world clock, so "24 a
  night" holds while they agree; sleeping through a night skips his hours and leaves the game day's count as it was.

  asking `GardenPacts.target` of them (the asking is spent only on an unanswered matter), so it may count a reserve he
  could not draw from anyway; answered "any full bed", each is asked as a source.
- **The room check is defensive:** with a bed's capacity 32, `SOURCE_MIN` 24 and `GAP` 3, a destination 3 below a source
  has room, so no test can reach the check alone.
