# Bathin's pact

Concordance garden cohort, GC-05 ([contract](concordance-garden-cohort.md#gc-05-bathin-unit-2); Dossier 2). Bathin, the
eighteenth spirit, sets Verdance down at once where it is wanted: from a Verdant Heart into a far Gleaner, or into a Ley
Pylon away from the beds, with nothing ever on the way. He is the cohort's second garden spirit, on the same pact record,
perch, screen, roster, journal and Jade lines as Stolas and Buer, and the first to raise a matter for his holder to answer.

## Player experience

- Understand **The Garden's Regard** (the codex's Binding category; the journal's pacts section names each next step).
  Its entry has four pages of his: "Bathin", "His Service", "Two Roads from One Heart" and "His Price".
- Build a **Pact Perch** where you may build, with an awake **Verdant Heart** of yours within 10 blocks and an awake
  **Gleaner** of yours, or a **Ley Pylon** of yours or no one's, within 10 blocks too, and use it. The perch first asks whom
  to invite: a card for each spirit you may invite (Buer shares The Garden's Regard), Bathin's with his portrait and
  "Ready", or locked with "Not yet: Needs Heart and Gleaner". Without them he does not come: "Nothing here to carry, or
  nowhere to carry it." Nothing is spent.
- Chosen, he is suddenly coiled on the perch, a broad-shouldered man on a serpent's tail, facing you, with puffs of dust at
  his coil. Three short beats (a Terms skip on the first two), then his terms as chips: any hour, 10 blocks, 12 a day, 4
  Verdant Chaff every 7 days with 4 ahead, a day's grace, you and your party, and the standing terms; the price as stacks,
  **2 ender pearls and 8 Verdant Chaff**, each you lack marked before Accept. Accepting spends them once, in the sealing
  step (no astrolabe, no resonance).
- **At any hour**, while you are within 64 blocks of the perch, he makes at most one move a second, 12 a game day, from an
  awake Heart of yours or your party's in his area: up to 8 Verdance into a Gleaner of yours or your party's more than 4
  blocks from that Heart, or else into a Ley Pylon of yours, your party's or no one's that is not beside it, at the Heart's
  own 3 for 2. From a Heart still beating on its bed he waits until it holds a whole move of 8; from one that has stopped
  he takes what is left. He points at where it is wanted and **both ends shine in the same moment**; nothing crosses
  between them and he holds none. The kept page counts today's moves ("1/12 today") and names his last ("Last: 8 Verdance
  to the Gleaner at x, y, z").
- **His matter, two roads from one Heart:** a Heart of yours that already pours into a Ley Pylon beside it he leaves alone
  and turns to once, weighing it. His kept page asks "Heart at x, y, z: take from it too?" with two plates, **Leave it** and
  **Take from it**; the roster and Jade say "awaiting your answer at its perch" until you answer. Your answer is kept at the
  perch at once, marked and changeable at any time; nothing is spent. Until you answer he keeps to Leave it and carries
  from your other Hearts.
- Four Verdant Chaff every 7 game days (up to 4 weeks paid ahead); a missed week is warned and he carries on through a game
  day's grace, then the pact is suspended with nothing lost; an offering resumes it. Your first task: read or set an awake
  living device (a Verdant Heart, Gleaner, Mulch Maw or Habitat Gauge) with an empty hand in his area while the pact is
  active.
- Ending at the perch (asked first) or from the roster returns what was paid ahead once; his coil tightens and he is gone,
  with puffs of dust and no path.

## Service: Verdance carrying (`verdance_carrying`)

- **Each think** (every 20 ticks) while his pact serves and his holder is within 64 blocks of the perch: at most one move,
  at most 12 a game day. His sources are the awake Hearts of his beneficiaries in his area (radius 10; at most 16, nearest
  the perch first, from his device index) holding a whole move of 8, or holding any Verdance and making no more (a Heart
  whose status is not `working`: full, starved, stalled, on no bed), the fullest first (GC-S25).
- **The lossless road first:** the emptiest Gleaner of theirs with room for 8 that is beyond that Heart's own reach (more
  than 4 blocks on some axis, so it would not draw there by itself): up to 8 through the Gleaner's own draw, both
  ownerships checked with the holder as the one acting.
- **Else the pylon road:** a Ley Pylon of theirs or no one's, not beside that Heart, with room for a batch: at most 3
  batches through the Heart's own pour (3 Verdance for 2 Ley Charge), its owner rule unchanged. A pylon no one owns is fed
  as the Heart's own pour feeds it, and asked as the holder's own.
- **One step:** both reservoirs change in one server call; nothing is ever on the way and he holds none. The move is
  counted on the record, he points at where it is wanted (his work gesture) and turns to it, and each end shows the shared
  work sign at once. No item, nutrient or practice moves; no Verdance is made.
- **Asked at the move:** both places through `GardenPacts.target` (serves there, a beneficiary's, the holder near, the
  holder's own hands), at most 8 places a think. A place refused for itself (a claim, a keeper no longer in the party) is
  not asked again until his index is next read (every 200 ticks), so refused places never crowd out the rest; when his
  questions run out he says why ("nowhere").
- **Bounds a day:** at most 96 Verdance into Gleaners (12 moves of 8), or at most 108 Verdance poured as 72 Ley Charge (12
  moves of 3 batches). From a Heart beating on its bed (1 or 2 Verdance a beat every 200 ticks) a move is 8 into a Gleaner,
  or usually 2 batches into a pylon (6 Verdance for 4 Ley Charge), 3 only when it held 9 or more.
- **Failure behaviour:** no Heart of his with a move to give: he waits (Jade: "Nothing to carry: the Hearts in his area hold
  no Verdance he may take"); nowhere to set it (every Gleaner full or within its Heart's reach, every pylon full or beside
  its Heart): he waits (Jade: "Nowhere to carry it: every Gleaner and pylon he may fill is full, or too near its Heart");
  a stranger's, a former party member's, or one the holder could not change by hand: skipped; unloaded: skipped (he loads
  nothing); holder away, dead, in another level or more than 64 blocks off: he waits (no stand-in); suspended or held:
  nothing; his day's moves spent: he stops till the next day; a Heart with its own pylon: left until answered.
- **First obligation (`DEVICE`):** read or set an awake Verdant Heart, Gleaner, Mulch Maw or Habitat Gauge with an empty
  hand in his area while the pact is active; recorded once (a waking does not count: a device placed by one who knows
  Verdant Husbandry is awake at once).

## His matter: two roads from one Heart (the accord)

A Verdant Heart that has a Ley Pylon beside it that it may feed (its keeper's party's or no one's, full or not) already
pours its Verdance into that pylon: it has a road. When such a Heart lies in his area and its keeper is one his pact
serves, Bathin raises it as a matter (`two_roads`) and leaves that Heart alone until his holder answers. Once a presence
he turns to the nearest such Heart with his inspect gesture. Until then he keeps to the safe course (Leave it) and
carries only from Hearts with no road of their own; nothing is lost, held or owed while the question waits.

- **Where it is asked.** On his kept page at the perch, a row, "Heart at x, y, z: take from it too?", and
  under it two plates, **Leave it** and **Take from it** (its whole sentence, "Your Heart already has a road.
  Shall I take from it too?", and that he keeps to Leave it meanwhile are the row's narration and hover). The roster says
  "awaiting your answer at its perch" with its waiting mark; Jade on the perch names the matter ("His matter: a Heart here
  already pours into a pylon of its own"), says the same waiting and counts the Hearts with their own road.
- **Answering.** Press a plate (keyboard: Tab to it, Enter). It is kept at once at this perch, marked with a tick and
  "Your answer: he keeps to this", and "Answer kept" shows on the page; Jade names it ("Its holder's answer: Leave it");
  he assents. It may be changed whenever the page shows it. Nothing is spent.
- **Take from it** (`claim`): he takes from that Heart too, to a far Gleaner or a far pylon, never to the pylon beside it.
  **Leave it** (`yield`) is also what an unanswered or unknown answer means.
- **Whose answer.** Only the pact's holder, in person, alive, at the perch, on the kept page that showed the answers;
  another player, a stand-in, a stale or out-of-range answer or another page is refused and nothing is written. A Heart
  whose keeper his pact no longer serves (a member who left the party) raises nothing and is not counted.
- **What is kept.** The answer is the perch's: one optional saved word, `accord` (`yield` or `claim`, at most 16
  characters, the perch's stamp still 1), written only while the pact is kept there. Ending the pact (at the perch or from
  the roster) or breaking the perch takes it away; a pact sealed or seated anew starts unanswered. A word this version
  does not know reads as unanswered and is kept as written until answered; an older build drops it, and the next build
  asks again.
- **Limits.** "Awaiting your answer" replaces the status his step returned only where `decide` returned idle, so an
  offering due is still said first, and it is reported only while his holder is near (away, the roster says so).

## Costs, balance and automation

He relaxes the Heart's adjacency layout without making anything: Verdance is conserved into a Gleaner and loses a third
into a pylon, at the Heart's own rate, so no cycle gains (`python3 tools/concordance_economy.py`: "gaining cycles: none";
no economy row added, since no conversion is new). Bounded: twelve moves a game day ([the service's bounds](#service-verdance-carrying-verdance_carrying));
the Heart's own pour into a pylon beside it stays the high-throughput layout, and he leaves such a Heart alone until his
holder answers. From a beating Heart he waits for a whole move (GC-S25), so his twelve moves are not spent on a beat's 1
or 2. His upkeep is the chaff the garden already leaves. Balance units: Verdance and Ley Charge, the Concordance's own
