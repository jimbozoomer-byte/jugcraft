# Industrial night vision metering

Owner-reported correction, November 3, 2026, implemented with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6). Follow-up to [powered armor feedback](powered-armor-feedback.md); the reported fast drain affects industrial goggles, nano helmets and quantum helmets. The exosuit's separate 2 JE/t price is retained.

## Cause and behavior

Industrial payment previously happened when the night-vision effect needed refreshing. The ownership state also compared the worn stack by Java object identity. Replacing a stack with an equivalent component copy, or deactivating and restarting vision, could discard that state and buy another 200 JE refresh. The earlier client test checked that energy was consumed but did not assert the exact consumption rate through these operations. Its 600-effect-tick server loop also did not represent 600 elapsed server ticks.

Each 200 JE payment now buys **160 active server ticks** of industrial vision, independently of the effect's 400/240 display refresh. The meter consumes at most one paid tick per player per overworld game tick, even when an equipment callback repeats. Equivalent stack copies retain the current effect. Already-paid industrial time survives effect replacement, bright/disabled pauses, unequipping and switching industrial helmet kinds; these operations do not purchase the same time again. Exosuit use does not consume industrial credit. An industrial helmet pays the next 200 JE only after the active budget is exhausted. External potion vision neither pays for nor consumes this budget.

The credit is a session-local player budget for one active vision effect, not JE stored in an item: no energy is added, refunded or duplicated, and two players cannot share it. Logout/respawn/server restart may discard unused credit; untracked saved effects retain the preceding behavior of fading naturally. No new saved component or migration is introduced. Existing industrial tooltip prices now say 200 JE per eight seconds of active vision. Other industrial abilities, including damage absorption and quantum breathing/cures, retain their own costs.

The existing JE producers, charging stations, recipes, modules and unlocks supply the same equipment. This repairs billing in the existing electrical progression without a new resource, conversion, automation loop, prerequisite, asset, dependency, renderer or packet. Other armor feedback and held-model fixes are preserved. No live-server deployment, restart or update-channel promotion is included.
