Preserve existing biome identities, use actual cave floors and Nether layers, reject unsupported/blocked growth and keep compost/bone-meal returns below their inputs. Huge glowcap targets: Fungal Thicket two attempts/layer, Glowcap Grotto two to four cave-floor attempts/chunk, Toadstool Field one/chunk, Mycelial Jungle 7%, Ender Wilds 5%. Blight targets: Blighted Sands one/layer, Withered Hollow three/layer, Rotted Expanse one/chunk on soul soil. Marrow Heap one/layer; brambles Netherbrush four/layer and Blighted Sands two/layer.

Batch 9b adds dragonblood timber, dark teal fronds, renewable End saplings, umbrella trees and shrubs, plus rare dye resin. Ender Wilds: 45% dragonblood, 20% shrubs, 30% jacaranda and 5% huge glowcaps within the existing two-to-three attempt budget. Outer Flats: one dragonblood attempt per ten chunks on end stone. Preserve end stone beneath growth; saplings need no skylight.
