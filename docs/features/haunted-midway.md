# Haunted Midway

Status: parts 1 (the sweet stalls) and 2 (the ticket booth and Whack-a-Zombie) implemented. CI builds them and runs their game tests and client screenshots; not yet played by hand. Parts 3 (the shooting gallery), 4 (the ghost train) and 5 (the haunted carousel) are planned.
Proposal issue: none. The owner asked for it directly on 28 October 2026, as set 6 of the ten sets of fall and Halloween decor ("Then I want you to do 1-10 in parts in that order one by one"), drawn by their rules.
Owner: @jimbozoomer-byte
Target milestone and tier: Discovery tier; made from wool, iron, a bucket, planks and a stick (the cart), and glass panes, a jack-o'-lantern, a cauldron, planks and iron (the wagon); part 2 from glass panes, a dispenser and planks (the booth), and torches, rotten flesh, a piston and planks (the game).
Primary specialty and supported player role: building, with a little cooking. A builder sets up a fair's midway after dark; the stalls make the sweets the kitchen already knows, at the same cost. Nothing in it is needed to progress.

## Player experience
The midway has its own livery, darker than the fall fair's red and white: midnight-violet lacquer and pumpkin-orange paint, gold trim and bone-white counters.

### Part 1: the sweet stalls
- **Cotton-Candy Cart** (one block across, two up): a cart the size of a real one.
  - Its violet cabinet is 69 cm wide and 53 cm deep, its counter 80 cm up, with a gold-beaded panel on each side. A paper cone of pink floss is painted on its front.
  - It stands on two orange-spoked wheels with gold hubs, and two iron legs at the front. A push handle with a wrapped grip runs out behind.
  - On its bone-white counter sits the steel spinning bowl: eight-sided, 53 cm across and 17 cm deep, with the motor's housing and the spinner head in its middle.
  - Two iron posts up its back hold a canopy at 1.7 m. Its striped valance hangs round it, ending in rounded tabs. Above, it steps up in tiers of umbrella panels, violet and orange by turns, to a gold cap and finial at 2 m.
  - Placed facing you, it needs the block above free.
  - **Its motor runs.** It is placed switched off; an empty hand switches it on or off, with a click. While it runs, **its spinner head spins** in the bowl and the heater band under the head glows orange.
  - **It spins sugar into floss.** Use sugar on it while it runs: the sugar is spent and spun into a pink ring of floss on the bowl's wall, deeper and higher with each serving, up to 3. Switched off, or with the bowl full, it says so and keeps the sugar.
  - **Paper winds a serving onto a cone**: the paper is spent and you get a Cotton Candy. It works whether the motor runs or not; with no floss in the bowl, it says so.
  - Recipe: three purple wool over an iron ingot, a bucket and an iron ingot, over a plank, a stick and a plank.
- **Cotton Candy** (a food): spun pink floss on a paper cone. It gives 2 hunger and a little saturation (0.1), as candy corn does. It is a candy (`c:foods/candy`) and composts a little.
- **Caramel-Corn Wagon** (one block across, two up): an old popcorn wagon.
  - Its violet cabinet is 75 cm wide, 53 cm deep and 63 cm high, on two big orange-spoked wheels 50 cm across and two iron legs. A striped popcorn box heaped white is painted on its front, and a push handle runs out behind.
  - On its counter stands a glass case 67 cm high in four gold corner posts. Inside it, a steel kettle hangs from two rods on a pivot, with its lid and the agitator's motor on top and a dump handle out behind. A lamp runs under the case's ceiling.
  - Its roof is in two striped tiers. A crest board on its front is painted with a black bat, and a jack-o'-lantern sits on it on a gold plinth.
  - **It lights.** It is placed out. Flint and steel or a fire charge lights it (the flint wears, the charge is spent), and a shovel puts it out. Lit, its case lamp and the lantern's face glow, and its upper block gives light 10.
  - **It pops corn.** Use corn kernels on it while it is lit: one kernel goes in the kettle and is spent.
    - The kettle crackles and jiggles on its pins while the kernel pops, and white flakes jump in the case.
    - After 3 seconds it has popped: **the kettle tips forward**, spilling it into the case, and swings back.
    - The case holds one more Popcorn, as smelting a kernel gives, up to 8. The heap rises with each.
    - It pops one kernel at a time; while it pops, or with the case full, or while it is out, it says so and keeps the kernel.
  - An empty hand takes a Popcorn out of the case.
  - **Caramel coats two into Caramel Popcorn**: use a Caramel on it with two or more in the case. The caramel is spent, two Popcorn go, and you get a Caramel Popcorn, as the crafting recipe (two popcorn and a caramel) gives. With fewer than two, it says so and keeps the caramel.
  - Recipe: a glass pane, a jack-o'-lantern and a glass pane, over a glass pane, a cauldron and a glass pane, over a plank, an iron ingot and a plank.
- Broken, either comes away whole, from either block, and drops itself once. The cart gives back a sugar for each serving left in its bowl. The wagon gives back the popcorn in its case, and the kernel in its kettle if one was popping.

### Part 2: the ticket booth and Whack-a-Zombie
- **Ticket Booth** (one block across, two up): a violet kiosk 87 cm wide, a black bat painted on its front.
  - A bone-white counter runs out before its ticket window, glazed in gold posts, at 87 cm. Its striped roof is in two tiers, and an orange crest board across its front is painted with a ticket.
  - On the counter stands a brass ticket dispenser, its slot to the front, a roll of paper on top and a crank at its side.
  - **Load the roll with paper**: each sheet is two tickets, up to 32. The roll shows on top of the dispenser, half full or full. Full, it says so and keeps the paper.
  - **An empty hand turns the crank** once round and prints a Midway Ticket. The crank turns once at a time; with nothing on the roll, it tells you to load it.
  - Recipe: three glass panes over a plank, a dispenser and a plank, over three planks.
- **Midway Ticket** (an item): a pumpkin-orange card ticket with a perforated stub. **It is a round's admission, not money**: printed from paper, it buys nothing but a round at the midway's games, and nothing turns it back. The mod's money stays the Jug.
- **Whack-a-Zombie** (one block across, two up): a game cabinet.
  - Its violet cabinet has a zombie's face painted on its front. Its orange playfield, at 94 cm, has five holes in black rubber rims.
  - A back board rises from the playfield's back edge, with eight score lamps along its top in gold sockets, a zombie's face painted under them and a striped crest with a bat.
  - **A Midway Ticket starts a round** of 20 seconds for whoever paid; a second ticket isn't taken while one runs. With no round on, an empty hand tells you how to start one.
  - **Zombie heads pop up**: square heads with dark hair, two black eyes and a frown, rising out of the holes one at a time, each from another hole than the last. Each stays up a second, with half a second between.
  - **Whack a head with a Carnival Mallet** (the High Striker's; left click, or use) to knock it down and light a lamp, with a thump.
    - Only the player who paid may whack, from within 6 blocks; another player is told it isn't their round.
    - A whack on the wrong hole is a miss and lights nothing.
  - **All eight lamps lit win a prize** from the midway's prize table (`jugcraft:gameplay/midway_prize`), as the High Striker's bell and Ring Toss do. It earns Step Right Up, and the round ends with a bell.
  - If the time runs out first, the round is lost with a low note. The lamps show the last round's score until the next.
  - Recipe: three torches over a plank, rotten flesh and a plank, over a plank, a piston and a plank.
- Broken, each comes away whole and drops itself once. The booth gives back a sheet of paper for each two tickets left on its roll; an odd one left over is lost.

## How it is drawn (docs/ART_DIRECTION.md, docs/NATURAL_TEXTURES.md)
- Built from boxes at real size, one texel to a model pixel, in the market displays' manner (`tools/haunted_midway_data.py`), and cut into each stall's two blocks' models.
- Each material is one colour stepped into tones, with its marks placed the way it is made:
  - **Lacquer**: a flat field in one tone inside a gold bead one texel in from its edge, lit along its top and left and shaded along its bottom and right.
  - **Stripes** two texels wide, violet and orange by turns, each lit along its first column. Each roof tier is set in by one stripe, so its stripes carry on the ones under it.
  - **The canopy's top** is an umbrella's panels seen from above: eight about its middle, violet and orange by turns, tier on tier about one middle. A valance's stripes end, two by two, in a rounded tab with its bottom corners cut out.
  - **Steel**: flat bands, lit along one edge. The bowl and the kettle are eight-sided: four sides square to the block and four turned 45 degrees, their floors the same eight sides (two crossed pairs of boxes).
  - **Floss**: pink strands wound on a slant, each row a step along, lit along its top row.
  - **Popcorn**: white puffs two texels square, lit at their top left, in a staggered grid with the golden hulls between them.
  - **Glass**, as the Caramel Apple Stand's jar: cut out inside a pale rim with a lit streak.
  - **The wheels** are the market's spoked wheels in the midway's livery: a black iron tyre of eight segments, orange spokes and a gold hub.
- **Signs are pictures, not lettering.** At a texel a pixel, a one-block cart has no room for a word, so the cart shows a cone of floss, the wagon a striped popcorn box and its crest a black bat.
- **Glows are steady and cut out**: the heater band, the case lamp and the lantern's face are full-bright and unshaded. The lantern's glow is cut out to the holes of its carved face.
- **Moving parts move.** The cart's spinner head and the wagon's kettle are each the blockstate's `runner` model, which a placed stall never takes:
  - `CottonCandyCartRenderer` spins the head at 18 degrees a tick about the bowl's middle while the motor runs;
  - `PopcornWagonRenderer` tips the kettle forward 40 degrees about its pivot and back over a second, smoothly, each time it spills, and jiggles it a degree or two while a kernel pops.
- The items are each stall whole at half size. The Cotton Candy's icon is a 16 by 16 map (`tools/item_icons/cotton_candy.txt`): a cloud of pink floss in two lobes, lit at its upper left, wound on a paper cone narrowing to its tip, in the upright family. Its floss is a new icon material (`floss` in `tools/icon_materials.py`).
- **The first previews**, all fixed before commit:
  - the canopy's and the roof's stripes, four texels wide on tiers set in by two, didn't line up from tier to tier and read as a jumble; they are now two wide with each tier set in by one stripe;
  - the canopy tiers' stripes turned a quarter on some tiers (a square piece may be laid either way in the texture); the canopy's top is now an umbrella's panels, which look the same either way, and stripes run along a piece's shorter side;
  - the canopy's two smallest tiers were too small for eight panels and read as noise; the top one is now a plain gold cap;
  - the signs' first pictures were wider than their panels and ran over the gold bead; they now fit inside it.

### Part 2's booth and game
- Built the same way, at real size (`tools/haunted_midway_data.py` ticket_booth(), whack_a_zombie()).
- **The booth**: the midway's lacquer and stripes; its window is the cut-out glass; the dispenser is brass in flat bands, its slot a dark strip. The paper on the roll is stripes along it, its ends rings of paper round a dark core. The crest is an orange board, lit along its top with a gold foot, painted with a bone-white ticket: a perforation down its stub and a black stamp.
- **The game**: its playfield is orange lacquer. Each hole is real geometry, not paint: an eight-sided black rubber rim round a darker well, so a head comes up exactly out of it.
  - The score lamps are bone-white bulbs in gold sockets, each lit as its own full-bright model from its score up, so they light in turn and never flash.
  - The heads are square, four pixels (25 cm): dark hair along the top in a diagonal weave, two black eyes set wide and a frown, on sickly green.
- **Moving parts move**, from each block's `runner` model:
  - `TicketBoothRenderer` turns the crank once round its axle, easing in and out, each time a ticket is printed;
  - `WhackAZombieRenderer` raises the head out of whichever hole is up, over a fifth of a second from when it popped.
- The Midway Ticket's icon is a 16 by 16 map (`tools/item_icons/midway_ticket.txt`): an orange card with its corners rounded, a notch in each end, a dotted perforation across its stub and two lines of black print, in the compact family. Its card is a new icon material (`ticket`), more yellow and saturated than copper so it can't read as metal.
- **The first previews**, all fixed before commit:
  - the game's first heads, three pixels square and green with black eyes, read as a Creeper's; they are now bigger, with dark hair, wide-set eyes and a frown;
  - the holes were narrower than the heads; they are now wider, and spaced so no two rims touch;
  - the ticket icon's first stamp, a bat at sixteen pixels, read as a heart and then a band of teeth; it is now two lines of print;
  - the icon's first orange was too near copper for the checker; it is now brighter.

## Connections
- Existing input producer: sugar and paper (vanilla, from sugar cane) for the cart; the farm's corn kernels (`jugcraft:corn_kernels`, from corn) and the kitchen's Caramel for the wagon.
- Existing output consumer: the Cotton Candy is eaten. The Popcorn and Caramel Popcorn are the kitchen's own, made as smelting and crafting make them: Caramel Popcorn goes on to the Popcorn Ball.
- Technology/magic connections (or infrastructure/cosmetic applicability): cosmetic, with the farm's corn and the kitchen's caramel.
- Reachable entry path: from the first day, with corn from the farm for the wagon.
- Which connections are required vs optional; trade and solo routes: all optional, crafted solo, and tradeable like any item. Popcorn is still made by smelting a kernel, and Caramel Popcorn by crafting, without the wagon.
- Part 2's inputs: paper for the tickets; the High Striker's Carnival Mallet to play. Its outputs: the midway's prizes (the plushes), from the prize table the High Striker and Ring Toss already roll.
- How this specialty stays useful without mastering every other branch: it is a fair's furniture for anyone's build.

## Balance and automation
- The cart costs three wool, two iron, a bucket, two planks and a stick; the wagon four glass panes, a jack-o'-lantern, a cauldron, two planks and an iron ingot. Each gives back only itself and what it holds.
- **The cart turns a sugar and a paper into one Cotton Candy** (2 hunger, 0.1 saturation): two sugar cane for a candy. Breaking it gives back the sugar still in the bowl, never more.
- **The wagon pops one Popcorn from one kernel**, exactly as smelting does, with no fuel, as a campfire cooks with none. Its Caramel Popcorn costs exactly the crafting recipe's two popcorn and a caramel. It never makes more than it is given.
- Breaking the wagon gives back exactly its popcorn and its one unpopped kernel.
- Part 2: a sheet of paper prints two tickets, and a round costs one. A won round gives one roll of the prize table, as a ringer or a rung bell does. The High Striker and Ring Toss stay free, so the ticket adds a little cost to the hardest game to win and no new prize.
  - Breaking the booth gives back a sheet for each two tickets left, never more; an odd ticket left over is lost.
  - Nothing turns a ticket or a prize back into paper, so there is no loop.
- No loop: nothing it makes leads back to sugar, paper, kernels or caramel (the audit refuses any loop).

## Multiplayer and persistence
- What each stall shows is its blocks' state, on both blocks, changed on the server only by the player using it, and saved with the world. The cart's is `lit` (its motor) and `floss` (0 to 3). The wagon's is `lit`, `popcorn` (0 to 8) and `popping`.
- **Popping is the server's**: a kernel put in schedules one block tick on the wagon's first block, 60 ticks later. That tick adds the popcorn and sends a block event, so every client near it sees the kettle tip. Nothing ticks otherwise.
- Each stall's block entity, on its first block (`cotton_candy_cart`, `popcorn_wagon`), keeps nothing: it is there for its renderer. The cart's spin is worked out from the game time; the wagon's tip from the time its block event was marked. The crackle and flakes are each client's own, near it, while a kernel pops.
- Part 2: the booth's roll is its blocks' state (`roll`, 0 to 32). The crank's turn is a block event its block entity (`ticket_booth`, keeping nothing) marks, and the next turn waits for it on the server.
- **Whack-a-Zombie's round is the server's**:
  - who paid and when the round ends are kept by its block entity (`whack_a_zombie`, saved as `player`, `ends` and `last`), so a round survives a restart;
  - the head that's up and the score are its blocks' state (`head` 0 to 5, `score` 0 to 8, `lit` while a round runs), so clients see them with nothing else sent;
  - the heads pop and drop on the first block's scheduled ticks, one at a time; nothing else ticks;
  - a whack is judged on the server: the player must be the one who paid, within 6 blocks and allowed to use blocks there, and the hole is found from where their swing (or use) landed, against the head's own shape.
- None of them can be pushed by a piston.
- IDs (stable from release):
  - the blocks and items `cotton_candy_cart` (`facing`, `part` 0 to 1, `lit`, `floss` 0 to 3, model-only `runner`; block entity `cotton_candy_cart`) and `popcorn_wagon` (`facing`, `part` 0 to 1, `lit`, `popcorn` 0 to 8, `popping`, model-only `runner`; block entity `popcorn_wagon`);
  - the item `cotton_candy`;
  - part 2's blocks and items `ticket_booth` (`facing`, `part` 0 to 1, `roll` 0 to 32, model-only `runner`; block entity `ticket_booth`) and `whack_a_zombie` (`facing`, `part` 0 to 1, `lit`, `head` 0 to 5, `score` 0 to 8, model-only `runner`; block entity `whack_a_zombie`, its saved `player`, `ends` and `last`), and the item `midway_ticket`.
- Their recipes follow the agriculture switch. The blocks and items stay registered when it is off.
