# Companion work feedback

Implemented with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) under the owner's standing model exception. Cosmetic feedback for the existing Peepo/Jughead job system; no new item, recipe, dependency, currency, energy cost or progression gate.

## Player behavior

Routine now includes **Feedback: Off / Subtle / Expressive**. Subtle is the default for existing and newly created companions. It shows persistent job problems, recovery, achieved stock targets and resolved problems. Expressive also shows occasional completed Output deliveries, actual bonus production and the harvest/catch/kitchen finishing touches described below. Alerts controls the quiet problem sound; Feedback Off also silences these work alerts. Existing Social controls greetings and conversations separately.

Four two-second clips share the current companion rig: a pleased one-arm fist pump with two upward strokes and happy nods, puzzled tilt and shrug, tired yawn with closed eyes and a hand toward the mouth, and a relieved hand-to-chest gesture with a small breath and slow nod. The unused arm stays relaxed in both positive reactions. Both variants and costumes use the same motions. Feet stay planted and arms keep their normal length. The gestures do not stop navigation or delay production. Work, cargo, held items, socializing, editing settings, eating, sleeping and movement take priority. A gesture can wait up to three seconds for a safe gap, then expires; it never interrupts work to ensure it gets shown.

Thought bubbles use native pixel geometry, symbols and item models. They appear above the head, jug and hats. Supplies show a specific ingredient only when an already selected plan unambiguously identifies it; alternatives use a question mark. Required shears, hoes, rods, buckets, bottles and selected seeds use their native item icon. Chest/exclamation, lightning, flame, question mark, Z, checkmark and bonus sparkle distinguish other events without relying only on color.

## Rules, privacy and server work

- Report changes come from the existing once-per-second cache. A problem must remain stable for three seconds. Unchanged problems do not repeat; problem/recovery events have a twenty-second cooldown and positive events a thirty-second cooldown.
- Active jobs and transport determine feedback. An inactive assigned job cannot override useful current activity. Growing crops, animal cooldowns, aging/drying, full generator buffers and internal scheduling waits remain calm. Resolved problems require stable productive work, not a budget wait or an unchecked readiness guess.
- Delivery feedback follows a committed transfer to Output. Bonus events follow the outer committed transaction for garden yields, catches, cutting-board/recipe-result duplication and cooking-pot bonuses. Rolled but aborted results cannot celebrate. Independently finishing skillet/stove batches retain their existing production behavior; their ordinary Output delivery can produce a delivery reaction, without guessing which nearby companion earned an earlier bonus.
- Reaction ID/start time and the optional registry ID of one displayed harvest/catch are public entity metadata. The latter is a cosmetic visible prop, never an item stack or its private components. Diagnostic kind and optional diagnostic item ID go only to tracking owners/authorized party members within sixteen blocks. No inventory components or inventory contents are broadcast. Permission revocation clears an active private bubble on the next half-second validation. Bubble data is ephemeral and never saved.
- Each client accepts at most six simultaneous thought bubbles, renders within twelve blocks, expires after two seconds and limits alert sounds to one per four seconds across nearby companions. Disconnection/world change clears the cache; UUID checks prevent entity-ID reuse from replaying another companion's event.
- No new entity, chest, recipe-catalog or path searches, chunk loads, autonomous goals or machine ticks are added. The current selected recipe is inspected only when emitting an ingredient bubble. New Routine data is appended to the existing menu and validated by its existing owner/party/distance checks.

## Persistence, assets and compatibility

`WorkFeedback` is an additive saved integer, clamped to the three supported modes. Missing data defaults to Subtle. Reactions, private bubbles and queued gestures do not survive reload. Existing assignments, inventory slots, stock settings and production behavior are retained. Server and clients must be updated together for the appended metadata and registered event packet.

The shared owner library and catalog were inspected for matching companion reaction assets. These clips extend the existing procedural rig under the framework catalog's allowance for simple existing renderers. No compatible supplied reaction clip was needed. Bubble shapes are authored geometry sampling the existing companion eye-white palette texel; item icons and the quiet pling refer to existing game resources. Opaque depth-writing geometry avoids symbol loss in the pack's transparency renderer. No third-party assets are copied or new assets/dependencies bundled.

### Job finishing touches, 26 October 2026

Implemented with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), extending the same feedback gate, entity metadata and procedural rig. Expressive adds three optional two-second clips for both companions and costumes:

- **Harvest inspection:** a slight downward look while turning the actual harvested produce between both hands. Garden crops, orchard fruit, mushroom colonies, dried herb bundles and magic crops feed the existing committed harvest path. Planting, fertilizing and failed/full-inventory harvests do not celebrate.
- **Catch admiration:** lift the actual landed loot in the left hand while the right keeps the rod. During the next fishing wait, the original seated legs, kicking, line and right-hand grip remain intact. A cast, bite or reel takes priority immediately. A catch can also be admired after fishing ends if both hands are otherwise free.
- **Brush off flour:** two palm-brushing strokes, a small head movement and four short-lived flour flecks. An actually assisted cooking-pot result, committed cutting-board/candy result, or successful food Output delivery from a cooking pot, hearth oven, cutting board, skillet, kitchen stove, candy kettle or canning kettle can request it. Whole baked pies and cakes are recognized through their native placed-food block type; they do not need an item `FOOD` component. Raw bakes, empty containers and burnt pies/cakes do not qualify as a successful kitchen finish. Plain porter trips and returned inputs retain their existing behavior. No chef uniform is required. A busy batch may skip the production gesture and show it after delivery instead.

Finishers share the thirty-second positive-event cooldown with deliveries, stock goals and bonuses; they do not add a second stream of celebrations. Each companion holds at most one pending finisher for three seconds, retaining the first result rather than extending the queue on every item. Admission is delayed until the pose can actually start. The hooks on transactional production run only on outer commit, including nested-transaction rollback. Existing generic reaction clips are unchanged.

Free-handed walking keeps its existing leg animation. Other work, carried cargo, held tools/lights, eating, recovery, sleeping, social poses and settings take precedence. The only work overlay is catch admiration during `FISH_WAIT`; it cannot own navigation, stall an AI goal, extend a recipe, stop a fishing timer, spend energy or reserve cargo. Displayed products are one registry item ID with no components, copied visually through native item models and positioned from the actual hands. They cannot be picked up or dropped. Prop/fleck rendering is local and limited to twelve blocks, with no new particle entities, searches, recipes, tasks, packet types or per-machine polling. Metadata is cleared on interruption and is never saved. Clients and server must update together.
