# Companion rest transitions and blankets

Owner requested 26 October 2026. Implemented with **OpenAI Codex (GPT-6)** on `peepo-companion`.

## Player behavior

Peepo and Jughead ease onto stools, supported player seats, bed edges and straight/diagonal fence rails, then resume their little leg kicks. Leaving reverses the seated pose with a short push and hop. Beds use a seated arrival, gradual recline, closed eyes and subtle breathing. Both hands pull the blanket toward the chest; waking opens the eyes, pushes the blanket toward the feet, sits upright and steps off. Existing Jughead/pumpkin pillow offsets, limb lengths and costume attachment are retained. Rest handling temporarily hides held props without changing the Hand slot or cargo.

Companion beds and vanilla beds supply their own dye color. The blanket references the native `minecraft:textures/block/<color>_bed_foot_up.png` fabric at runtime, matching the existing bed models and resource-pack replacements. It is a small rendered sheet with side drapes, a folded edge and a covered foot end, not an item or entity. It covers the body, legs and feet below the face; Jughead keeps his jug. There is no blanket recipe, consumption, drop or inventory slot.

Upper bunks use the existing checked bottom entrance. The visible companion climbs along that front column, turns onto the assigned bunk and settles. Waking reverses the route. The access column is briefly reserved during climbing; an occupied bunk retains its reservation through a normal visual departure. Other sleepers keep their own bunk. Invalid furniture, command/settings changes, damage, eating, capture and unloading cancel the presentation; normal departures retain a short movement pause while stepping off. A blocked safe exit retains the furniture reservation and movement hold while using the existing exit retry. If safety selects a different exit or another climber owns the access column, the cosmetic departure is skipped at the safe server position.

Recovery stays at 8 reserve JE/t sitting and 16 JE/t sleeping at night, with the existing food bonus, schedules and Overworld-only nighttime rule. Core rest clips are independent of Routine feedback; existing tired yawns retain their original feedback settings and cooldowns. No new energy source, progression gate or productive job is introduced.

## Implementation

`CompanionRestMotion` publishes one transient furniture snapshot: clip, timestamp, anchor, local entry/mount offsets, facing, dye and climb duration. Normal occupancy renewal does not restart it. Relative offsets preserve coordinate precision away from spawn. The server retains the established authoritative furniture position, collision checks and safe exit; clients draw the short furniture-relative travel from the snapshot. Normal departure uses the existing Rest goal to hold movement until the clip finishes. Arrival is 14 ticks for seats, 24 for beds; departures are 12/24 ticks, plus 14 ticks per bunk level (bounded at 160 ticks). Transition cancellation releases its temporary reservations.

`RestPose` evaluates the shared phases for the rig, position and `RestBlanketLayer`. Arms retain native length; the blanket's moving fold follows the actual hands. The renderer includes the access column in its culling bounds. Clients tracking an already resting companion sample the current phase instead of replaying arrival. No extra pathfinding, world search, recipe scan, per-frame packet, item copy ownership or prop entity is introduced. Exit clips check their loaded anchor every five ticks; ordinary beds/seats keep their existing validation. No transient animation is saved. Existing saved safe-exit data still prevents a reloaded companion becoming stranded in a bunk. Client and server should update together.
