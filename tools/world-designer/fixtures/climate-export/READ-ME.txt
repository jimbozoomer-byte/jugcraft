Requires Jugcraft with World Designer and the same Minecraft/mod collection used to export the catalog.
Create a NEW world; add this ZIP in Data Packs, enable it, then choose Jugcraft Designed in World Type. Keep structures enabled.
The painted surface sits above original deep caves; outer two grid cells blend to procedural terrain.
Structure pins choose start chunks, not exact building corners; biome and terrain constraints still apply. Native random structures remain enabled. One Jugcraft walled city is supported.
Do not replace this pack in an established world: already generated chunks are not regenerated.
