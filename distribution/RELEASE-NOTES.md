# JUGCRAFT · Winter Market and the Western City

Local release candidate `0.1.0-alpha.20261118.1`. These changes are measured from the published `0.1.0-alpha.20261103.1` pack.

<!-- player-notes -->
- Visit three winter-market stalls on the harbour quay once the site opens. Inga runs the green parcel booth, Oskar the red bakehouse and Marta the blue toy stall. Each counter also has a crafting recipe for home use.
- Hold a stack at a wrapping counter, pick a player and choose Wrap & post. Your friend can collect it from any wrapping counter, even if they were offline when you sent it.
- Choose Wrap & carry for a named present you can hand over or tuck under a tree. Only the addressee can unwrap it. If it gets lost, they can collect the same gift at the counter. Signed books, names, enchantments and stack counts travel with the gift.
- Pick up three gingerbread cookies or two cider doughnuts for 1 Jug, or a mulled cider for 2. The toymaker sells a spinning top for 2 Jugs, a turkey pull toy for 3 and a Mayflower model for 5.
- Striped awnings, shelves of parcels and toys, a copper drinks urn, wreaths, warm string lights and icicles dress the quay. A five-block promenade keeps a clear path to the water.
- After Thanksgiving weekend, a 31-block Christmas fir takes over the original city square, with eight decorated tiers, a lit star and room for sixteen public gifts. In 2026 the default season runs November 30 through January 6. Outstanding gifts stay collectible afterward.
- Craft a five-block Harbour Gift Fir for your own gathering. Crouch and use the tree with a stack to leave a public present, or use an empty hand to collect one.
- Light a lane with three-block peppermint lamps or use five-block boulevard lamps to frame a gateway. Decorate the ground with wind-carved drifts and shovelled snow, and hang solid icicle fringes under the eaves.
- Furnish an Icelandic hot spring with joinable benches, peg rails and boot trays. The pool kit includes a dipper pail, pool lantern and a gated overflow spout that runs when supplied with water.
- Read the server news in the Town Dispatch when you join or with /bulletin. Open one free parcel per UTC day for useful supplies. A full inventory lets you return later for the same roll.
- The western city expansion adds a second walled ring across a bridge from the original west gate. The new districts open after staff survey and install them in the existing world.
- Brick, Bell and Timber Courts have 72 rentable apartments across three buildings of eight, seven and six storeys. Visit a building manager or use /apartment list to find a room.
- Rent a room for seven real days for 8 or 12 Jugs and renew it explicitly. Decorate with supported furniture, lamps, carpets and private storage. Former tenants can reclaim belongings after staff clear an expired unit.
- Staff can designate new rental interiors in game. Apartment boundaries protect walls, neighbouring rooms, doorways and public stairs.
- Follow the covered bridge passage down to Deepstead. Three connected mine galleries sit 54, 68 and 82 blocks below the old city, with workshops, a watchtower, market and glazed waterworks.
- Meet Deepstead’s dwarven workers, merchants and guards. Visit the lampwright for a three-setting geode lamp and playable gallery chimes. The miners’ outfitter sells a plummet and survey reel.
- Dagna’s ten Stonewright’s Hall wares include border pavers, a keystone, a scrollwork corbel, a working stonecutting bench, a cavity-sounding hammer, a spirit level, a span rule and a light meter.
- Bruni’s Copper Kettle has ten kitchen goods, drinks and dishes. Try ember-onion pasties, saltcap skewers, golden leek tarts, a nausea-clearing caveberry shrub or Glowroot Steep for night vision. The samovar steams and the shift gong rings.
- West Gate Harbour has three piers, two boardable display ships, a fish market, cargo derrick, lighthouse and customs belfry. New guards patrol Old Town and the western approaches.
- Four Old Town buildings gain upper floors and compact counterweight lifts. The West Gate House has a chart room and copper-trimmed hoist. The South Square Inn has three extra projecting storeys above its first raised floor and a furnished guest chamber.
- Ride Icelandic, Mongolian, Shire, Giant Finnhorse and Friesian horses with distinct travel, health and hauling strengths. Crossbreeding records ancestry.
- Tame a Pegasus and fly with animated wings. Use Space to climb and Left Ctrl to descend. Shires, Giant Finnhorses and saddled Pegasi can carry breed-sized cargo bags.
- Encounter pirate crews patrolling the open sea aboard their ships. The update also adds early firearms and Thanksgiving-era weapons.
- Decorate for Thanksgiving with feast dishes, homestead and woodland crafts, toys and festival pieces. Town placements account for player additions.
- Town guests have more friendship activities, errands and wardrobe options. Marriage uses the reviewed adult roster.
- Peepos have better work orders, batching, Staff Posts, harvesting and social animations. Companions gain garden pacts.
- Explore first-stage star-bred animal lineage with the Astrawand. Pharmacy progression adds treatments and an on-screen strain indicator.
- Powered night-vision goggles apply night vision while charging for use. Powered tools gain charging and handling fixes, including scanner cancellation, chainsaw costs and drill actions.
- Lapis now goes into the Imbuement Chamber to make source gems. The Hydroponics Bay explains its Nutrient Solution requirement and accepts that solution by pump, pipe or bucket.
- Fixed a villager-trading tooltip crash. Long item descriptions wrap on small screens, and native inventory tooltips render properly.
- Aircraft have control, banking, seating, muzzle-alignment and compact HUD fixes. Walkers gain cockpit, aiming and safe-dismount improvements.
- Raiders retry blocked approaches and guards have better lookout behaviour around the city walls.
- Tech and magic share encyclopedia progression routes. Restored introductory chapters and boss dossiers explain preparation, arena entry, rewards, resets and exits.
- Encounter the Storm Roc and Glass Abbot. The Cinder Tyrant gains heat and cooling mechanics. Migrated lair keepers recognise support contributions. The Roc tracks damage and lightning-rod participation separately.
- More armour sets, blank runes, Immolation and Drygmy’s Blessing have survival sources. Prepared-component recipes give rewards and mineral-sand processing is simpler.
- Make plastic from naphtha or scavenged plastic. Copper and iron cables now use their intended capacities.
- Seasonal quests, boss bounties and Beardshy join the adventure. Mushroom and fairy-ring ground cover is less dense.
- Imported models and textures, storage-cell indicators, connected furnishings and item names have been repaired. More decorative materials work with transmutation.
- On worlds using the new climate source, future Overworld terrain follows latitude from spawn. Travel roughly 2,500 blocks north for arctic country. Head south for hot and tropical terrain past 2,000, temperate forests and grasslands past 4,000 and frigid biomes past 5,500. Bands blend at their borders.

The release keeps Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 and Java 25. The existing-world climate source needs an offline migration if the save still uses the original source. The waterfront and existing Old Town buildings have their own surveyed installation steps. The market template needs a clear site before placement.

Draft PR #612 remains outside this release pending review of the world-height change, TV helpers and other content. Local packaging does not publish the player channel or install the server update.
