against them. The library's [use terms](../README.md#allowed-project-use) apply.

The only change to a page is in its bottom-left corner, where the owner had scribbled out some text in red before sharing
it: that corner (x 3 to 139, y 504 to the bottom) is painted over with the page's own background grey, row by row, so
nothing of the scribble or what was under it is kept.
