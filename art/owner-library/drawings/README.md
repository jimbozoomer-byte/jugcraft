# The owner's drawings

Pages the owner drew and shared for Jugcraft, kept as they were shared so the content rebuilt from them can be checked
against them. The library's [use terms](../README.md#allowed-project-use) apply.

| Page | Shared | What it shows | Built from it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [cakes_and_bakes.png](cakes_and_bakes.png) | 8 October 2026 | "CAKES & BAKES - 3D CAKES": a carrot cake (whole, and cut, "INTERIOR"), a birthday cake, an ice cream cake, a red velvet cake, a cheesecake, a coffee cake and an apple cake, drawn as blocks seen from above the south-east | The cakes ([docs/features/cakes.md](../../../docs/features/cakes.md)): `tools/cake_art.py` reads their textures off this page |
| [pies_and_tarts.png](pies_and_tarts.png) | 8 October 2026 | "CAKES & BAKES - 3D PIES & TARTS": a strawberry pie and a blueberry tart drawn large, each with a quarter cut away, and a plum pie, a banoffee pie, a sweet berry tart, a golden tart (labelled "PORK PIE"; the owner named it the Lemon Tart), a pumpkin pie, a pork pie, a strawberry tart and a coffee tart, drawn as blocks seen from above the south-east | The pies and tarts ([docs/features/pies-and-tarts.md](../../../docs/features/pies-and-tarts.md)): `tools/pie_tart_art.py` reads their textures off this page |

The pages are changed in one place each, and the pies and tarts page in one more. In each bottom-left corner the owner
had scribbled out some text in red before sharing it: that corner (on the cakes page x 3 to 139, y 504 to the bottom; on
the pies and tarts page x 11 to 130, y 512 to the bottom) is painted over with the page's own background grey, row by
row, so nothing of the scribble or what was under it is kept. On the pies and tarts page the white strip down its left
edge (x 0 to 10) is kept white beside it, and an image viewer's arrow button over the page's left side (x 14 to 58, y
263 to 307) is painted over the same way.
